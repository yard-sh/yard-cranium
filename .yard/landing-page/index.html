How it works

Three steps. No setup.

  1. 01

    Sign in, get a workspace

    Your personal workspace is waiting the moment you arrive. Make more for each team, trip or side quest.

  2. 02

    Share one link

    Every workspace has a single invite link. Whoever opens it and signs in is in. Reset it and the old one stops working.

  3. 03

    Write at the same time

    Open a document together and just type. Everyone sees every keystroke, with a little flag saying whose it is.

Built on a CRDT

Edits merge. They never collide.

Under the hood every document is a Yjs CRDT, a data structure made for many writers at once. Two people typing into the same sentence both keep their words. There's no lock, no "someone else is editing", and no conflicted copy.

  • Drop your connection mid-sentence: your edits wait, then catch up.
  • Undo takes back your own changes, never somebody else's.
  • The first line is the title. The grid of documents updates itself.

Workspaces

A door you control.

Documents live in workspaces, and whoever makes a workspace owns it. The owner sees who's in, hands out the invite link, and can show anyone the door.

  • Removing someone closes their open documents on the spot, not at their next refresh.
  • Reset the invite link in one click. The old one stops working at once.
  • Hop between workspaces from the sidebar. Your personal one is always there.
